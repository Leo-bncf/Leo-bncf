## What I’m building

### <a href="https://schedual-pro.com/"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/SCHEDUAL-3157D5?style=for-the-badge" alt="Schedual" /></a> · Timetabling for real curricula

Schedual helps schools generate a whole-school timetable that respects student choices, curriculum rules, teacher availability and room capacity. It began with the IB, including DP HL/SL combinations, shared classes and the core, and is designed to support other curricula too.

As a co-founder, I work across the product from understanding school workflows to building the software. My focus includes the **constraint-optimisation engine**: translating curriculum and school rules into a model the solver can use, then connecting that work to the product schools use. I’m also involved in the website, front end and backend.

### <a href="https://scholr.pro/"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/SCHOLR-159477?style=for-the-badge" alt="Scholr" /></a> · School management for international schools

Scholr brings core school workflows into one platform: student records, class lists, gradebooks, attendance, messaging, timetables and portals for students and families. For IB schools, it also accounts for **CAS, the Extended Essay and TOK**, while supporting schools that run multiple curricula and grading systems.

I’m helping shape Scholr from the product idea through to the software and user experience. The key challenge is making the platform fit how schools actually work across the **PYP, MYP and Diploma Programme**, while keeping its structure flexible enough for international schools with more than one curriculum.
